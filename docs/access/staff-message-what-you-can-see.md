# What you can see

Welcome to the hockey operations app. This note tells you what you can open, what you
can't, and who to ask. You'll never be asked for your password by email or chat. Sign
in only on the app's sign-in page.

## If you are an assistant scout

**You can see:**
- scouting notes and game observations from our scouts (goals, assists, shots, hits,
  penalties, and written notes);
- the game schedule;
- the player list: name, position, team, and sweater number.

**You can do:** add your own scouting notes from games you watch, and fix notes you wrote.
You can't change notes written by anyone else.

**You cannot see:**
- players' medical or health records;
- injuries;
- training and fitness numbers (sports science).

These stay with the medical staff.

**On shared scouting or game screens:** health or injury notes will **not** appear there
either. The player list and player pages won't tell you who is hurt or not cleared to
play. If you need to know whether a player is available, ask the hockey operations lead.

## If you are a video analyst

**You can see:**
- scouting notes and game observations from our scouts, so you can match video moments
  to what they recorded;
- the game schedule;
- the player list: name, position, team, and sweater number.

**You can do:** read only. You can't add or change scouting notes.

**You cannot see:**
- players' medical or health records;
- injuries;
- training and fitness numbers (sports science).

These stay with the medical staff.

**On shared scouting or game screens:** health or injury notes will **not** appear there
either. The player list and player pages won't tell you who is hurt or not cleared to
play. If you need to know whether a player is available, ask the hockey operations lead.

## If you already work here

Nothing about your own work has changed, with one exception: **the player list and
player pages no longer show who is on injured reserve.**

### Coaches

- **You can see:** the game schedule and the player list.
- **You cannot see:** scouting notes, medical or health records, injuries on shared
  screens, or training and fitness numbers.

### Scouts

- **You can see:**
  - all scouting notes and game observations, and you can add and fix your own;
  - the game schedule and the player list.
- **You cannot see:** medical or health records, injuries on shared screens, or training
  and fitness numbers.

### Medical and sports-science staff

- **You can see:**
  - players' medical and health records, and training and fitness numbers, which you can
    add and update;
  - the game schedule and the player list.
- **You cannot see:** scouting notes.

## If you are not sure

If you think you should see something you can't, or you see something you think you
shouldn't, **stop and tell the hockey operations lead.** Don't try to find another way
in. Don't share your sign-in with anyone, and don't ask a colleague to look something up
for you.
